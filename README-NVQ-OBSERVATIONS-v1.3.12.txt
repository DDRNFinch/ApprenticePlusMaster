Apprentice+ v1.3.12

- Learning outcome tick boxes now work in Assessor Observation and Witness Testimony.
- Any combination of outcomes may be selected; all outcomes are not required.
- Selected outcomes generate an editable observation or witness testimony of approximately 200 words.
- Learner name is used naturally and not in every sentence.
- At least one outcome, narrative, activity, name and signature are required to submit.
