Apprentice+ v1.3.11 — Optional Unit Selection

Changes:
- The four optional units are now one Evidence Pack 9.
- Learner selects Unit 238, 690, 828 or 837.
- The selected unit is saved locally and becomes Evidence Pack 9.
- Only the selected unit contributes to course progress and evidence.
- Developer Mode can reset the choice before Evidence Pack 9 evidence is added.
